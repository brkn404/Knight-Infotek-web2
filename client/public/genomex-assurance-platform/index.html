The assurance gap

Data has become infrastructure. Trust has not.

Recovery points, corpora, training sets, releases, and agent actions now participate in operations. Existing tools answer availability, detection, and vulnerability — not whether the asset should proceed at the moment of use.

Re-infection fear

A hardened repository can faithfully preserve ransomware. A clean recovery point can sit on a drifted backup platform.

Validation does not scale

Physical clean rooms work for one critical workload. They do not rank hundreds of recovery points after an incident.

No portable proof

DORA, HIPAA, cyber insurance, and internal audit need artifacts — not an administrator's opinion.
